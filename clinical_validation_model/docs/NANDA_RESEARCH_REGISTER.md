## Safe implementation interpretation

1. Store only edition/source metadata and synthetic opaque references until a written digital-use license is documented.
2. Do not name a product or feature in a way that implies INKA endorsement or affiliation.
3. Do not treat NANDA evidence levels, article accuracy estimates, or expert-consensus scores as `clinical_validation_score` or diagnosis probability.
4. A licensed rule still requires population, setting, temporal, measurement, and independent clinical review before activation.
5. Required pre-operation state transition:

```text
RESEARCH_ONLY
  -> LICENSE_REVIEW_REQUIRED
  -> CLINICAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED
  -> APPROVED_FOR_TEST
  -> APPROVED_FOR_OPERATION
```

Until every transition is evidenced, the rule remains `BLOCKED_BY_LICENSE` or `BLOCKED_BY_CLINICAL_REVIEW`.
